This study developed a machine learning classification model to predict whether a patient is diabetic based on medical measurements. Data cleaning was performed by replacing medically unrealistic zero values with median values. Exploratory Data Analysis revealed that glucose concentration, BMI, serum insulin, age, and pregnancy count are important variables associated with diabetes.
